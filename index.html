OnoDocs samples

Render Word documents, add application controls, fill tagged fields, and inspect document structure.

  • Viewer — open a document, zoom, and export a page.
  • Custom form — attach HTML inputs and collect values.
  • Template — fill a booking confirmation.
  • Document workflows — tables, ASCII, document tree, and a simulated email draft.

These examples run locally in evaluation mode. The SDK adds an evaluation watermark. Selected documents stay in your browser.
